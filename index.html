Privacy Policy

Effective: 10 October 2026 | Android package: com.safetywalk.app

VeriQ is an audit and inspection productivity app operated by Techno Fleet Labs. This policy describes the current free-launch version, including safety walks, site audits, photo evidence, signatures, drafts and PDF reports. Privacy and support contact: kanthinaveen@proton.me.

Information we process

  • Accounts: name, email, user identifier, role and account status. Passwords are submitted to Supabase Authentication for signup and sign-in. Session tokens keep you signed in.
  • Inspection records: manually entered site name, visit date and time, shift, auditor or inspector name, site manager or lead name, checklist wording, answers, remarks, corrective actions and scores.
  • Signatures: optional handwritten strokes, signer name and signing time. These are inspection records, not biometric identity verification.
  • Evidence: photos you capture or select for your checklist.
  • Technical information: our hosting and authentication providers receive network information such as IP addresses and request metadata to operate and secure the service. Their operational logs and backups follow their applicable service settings and retention practices.

The app does not request GPS location in this release. A site name you enter is not background location tracking. Avoid including unnecessary personal, sensitive or confidential information in inspections or photographs.

How information is used

We use information to provide accounts, control access, save and synchronize your current inspection, calculate scores, generate reports, retain local drafts, and handle support or deletion requests. Administrators can manage account names, emails, roles and enabled status.

Cloud and local storage

Supabase provides authentication and the cloud database. Account information and your current inspection state, including entered names, answers and signature data, are sent to Supabase over HTTPS. Supabase processes this information as our service provider; see Supabase's privacy policy.

Evidence image files, saved drafts and generated PDF reports are stored locally in browser storage or the app's private phone storage. Image references may be synchronized, but this release does not upload the evidence image files to the cloud database. Local files can be lost if app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Operating-system backups may behave differently depending on your device settings.

When you save or share a report, it may contain names, inspection details, signatures and photos. You choose its destination. Copies exported to another app, storage service or recipient are outside our control.

Ads and purchases

Report downloads are free in this release. Go Pro purchases and rewarded ads are disabled. Integrations for future Google AdMob ads and RevenueCat subscriptions are included, but advertising and purchase flows are not invoked in the free-launch configuration. We will update this policy and relevant disclosures before enabling those features.

Retention and account deletion

Account information and synchronized inspection state remain while your account is active, unless removed earlier. Local drafts, evidence and reports remain until you delete them or clear app/browser storage. Signing out does not erase local records.

You can request deletion of your account and associated cloud data from Request account deletion in the app menu or sign-in page, or follow the instructions on our account-deletion page. Email kanthinaveen@proton.me from your registered email, with the subject VeriQ account deletion. Never send your password. We may verify ownership before processing the request.

Deletion removes your authentication account, profile, synchronized current inspection and associated download-accounting records from the active database. Disabling access alone is not deletion. Local and exported copies must be removed separately. Backups and security logs are not necessarily erased at the same time as active records. If any information must be retained for a legal or security obligation, we will explain the information, reason and applicable retention period when handling your request.

Security and your choices

VeriQ uses HTTPS for backend communication and account-based database access controls. No system guarantees absolute security. Protect your device, credentials and exported reports. Contact us for privacy, access, correction or deletion questions.

Age requirements

VeriQ is intended for users aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. Follow local minimum-age and parental-consent requirements where applicable. Contact our privacy email if a child under 13 has provided personal information.

Website hosting and changes

This public policy site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and technical request information; see GitHub's privacy statement. We may update this policy when processing practices change. The effective date appears above.
