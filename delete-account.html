Delete your VeriQ account

You can request deletion without installing the app or signing in.

Send a deletion request

  1. Send an email from your registered VeriQ account email to kanthinaveen@proton.me.
  2. Use the subject VeriQ account deletion and ask us to delete your account and associated cloud data.
  3. Support may verify ownership, then confirm deletion or explain any information that must be retained.

Open email request

This button opens an email draft. Send the email to submit your request. It does not delete your account immediately. Never include your password, verification code or payment information. If an email app is unavailable, use your email website and the address above.

What is deleted

Your authentication account, profile (name, email, role and status), synchronized current inspection (including entered names, responses and signatures), and associated download-accounting records are removed from the active database. You will no longer be able to sign in to that account.

Local files and exported reports

Support cannot remotely erase copies stored on your phone, browser, another device, or a report recipient's storage. Delete saved drafts and reports from VeriQ's menus, and clear app/browser storage to remove remaining locally held data, including evidence images. Delete exported PDFs and backups separately. Save any reports you need before clearing storage.

Retention exceptions

Backups and security logs may persist according to provider retention settings. If a legal or security obligation requires retention of any information, support will explain what is retained, why and the applicable period when processing your request. Account disabling is not account deletion.

For privacy questions or other data requests, email kanthinaveen@proton.me. See our Privacy Policy.
